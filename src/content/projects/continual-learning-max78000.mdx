Bachelor's thesis project (University of Trento, 2022): applying continual learning to a microcontroller — the Maxim Integrated **MAX78000**, which ships with an on-chip CNN accelerator.

## What it does

- A C++/Python program that runs a neural network on the MAX78000 to recognise MNIST digits (0-9).
- Uses the chip's CNN accelerator for real-time, on-device learning rather than pure inference.
- Teaches the network a brand-new class (the letter "A") in under a few milliseconds, reaching 97% accuracy.
- Logs power draw during learning and visualises it with NumPy, pandas and Matplotlib.

## Why it matters

Continual learning on full-size GPUs is one thing; doing it on a microcontroller with kilobytes of memory, in real time, is a much harder constraint-fitting exercise — and a good preview of what edge-AI hardware can actually do today.
